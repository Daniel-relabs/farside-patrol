Year 2291, the Kessra system

Farside Patrol

The colony on Kessra IV sits at the edge of charted space, a thin line of lights between a ringed giant and the dark.

For three months, raider packs have hunted the supply lanes above the planet. The freighters stopped coming. The colony is running out of time.

You are the last patrol pilot at Farside Station. Your fighter is fuelled on pad 1 and cleared for launch.

Spool the engines, climb through the atmosphere and hold orbit against everything they send. Every wave you survive pays 100,000 pennies.
